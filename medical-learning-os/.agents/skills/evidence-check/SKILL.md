# Evidence Check

Educational research support, not patient-specific medical advice.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current specialty-society guideline or government health-agency guidance.
2. Original randomized trials, systematic reviews, or meta-analyses.
3. High-quality peer-reviewed review articles.
4. Standard medical textbooks for stable foundational physiology.

Do not use search snippets, social posts, or an AI answer as evidence. State plainly when evidence is weak or conflicting.

## Workflow

1. Restate the request as one checkable claim.
2. Determine whether it is foundational/stable or time-sensitive/clinical.
3. Search authoritative sources for current, clinical, quantitative, guideline-based, or uncertain claims.
4. Conclude: **supported**, **probably supported**, **uncertain**, **unsupported**, or **contradicted**.
5. Identify population, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, and key limitations where relevant.
6. Separate the evidence from common USMLE simplifications.
7. Link direct sources and guidance dates/versions.
8. State what the evidence does not establish.
