# Medical Learning OS instructions

This repository supports medical education and USMLE-style study. It is not a clinical decision-support system.

## Skill routing

- Use `medical-safety` whenever a request involves symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, doses, a real patient, an urgent situation, or medical evidence.
- Use `usmle-tutor` for mechanism-based concept teaching and active-recall quizzes.
- Use `question-review` after missed, guessed, or slow practice questions.
- Use `evidence-check` before presenting current medical facts, guidelines, statistics, therapies, or claims of benefit/harm.
- Use `anki-builder` when converting notes or reviewed concepts into flashcards.

## Source and learning rules

- Separate established facts, exam simplifications, and uncertainty.
- Prefer primary literature, specialty-society guidelines, government health agencies, and recognized textbooks.
- Cite direct sources when browsing is used.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted question-bank text or explanations. Summarize the learning objective in original language.
- Do not store patient-identifiable information.
